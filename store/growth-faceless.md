# Tarima growth — faceless execution (no founder on camera)

Addendum to `growth-plan.md`. Constraint: the founder will **not** appear in
videos. Good news — the plan barely changes, because its engine was always
"seed creators who ARE the faces" + "the app screen is the content." This doc
rewrites the parts that assumed a founder face, and lists only faceless formats.

## Why no-face doesn't break the loop

The growth plan's compounding mechanism is *other people* filming themselves with
the app and getting reposted. Those creators are the faces. The founder's real
jobs are **recruiting them and reposting fast** — both faceless, both suited to a
sales-strong operator. The only thing we drop is "founder reps to camera," which
was never the load-bearing part.

## The content the brand account posts (all faceless)

Every format below is a 9:16 screen-recording of a Tarima session (beat playing,
constraint words dropping on the beat). No face, no founder voice required.

1. **Reto / "¿tú rimarías esto?"** — the screen shows the beat + the words
   appearing. Caption challenges the viewer to rhyme them. The viewer is the
   performer; the comments fill with attempts. This is the primary faceless
   format and it drives participation, not just views.
2. **Palabra imposible** — escalate the constraint word to something absurd
   (`esternocleidomastoideo`). On-screen text only: "rimá esto en 4 barras."
   Comedy + challenge, zero performance.
3. **Niveles (fácil → extremo)** — fast montage of the same beat at four
   difficulties, words getting harder. Pure screen capture; text overlay labels.
4. **Repost (creator-supplied face)** — the best #RetoTarima entries. The face
   is theirs. Collab/repost so it hits both audiences. This is the bulk of the
   "people" content.
5. **Diccionario de rimas demo** — type a hard word, show the consonant/asonante
   rhymes fan out. Useful, screen-only, evergreen "como rimar X" search bait.
6. **ASMR-beat / loop** — a clean 15s of the pentagram metronome + a boom-bap
   loop. Low effort, surprisingly sticky as a "practice with me" background clip.

Production: these are literally screen recordings of the app (iOS/Android screen
record) + a caption + the beat as audio. A batch of 15 can be made in an
afternoon with no on-camera anything.

## What the founder actually does (faceless workload)

- **Recruit** 50 micro-creators (DM, personal Pro code). Sales work.
- **Repost** every decent entry within 24h. Speed = the reward.
- **Batch-record** ~15 screen clips, schedule 1/day.
- **Reply** to comments with a follow-up clip (still faceless — another screen
  capture answering "ok pero ¿y esta palabra?").

No part of this requires the founder's face or voice.

## Adjusted 6-week run sheet (faceless)

- **Wk 0:** deploy redeem-code backend (`redeem-backend.gs`) + set
  `NEXT_PUBLIC_REDEEM_URL`; create @tarimafreestyle; batch-record 15 faceless
  clips; build the 50-handle seed list (`outreach/seed-list.csv`); finalise DM
  template (`outreach/dm-templates.md`).
- **Wk 1:** post 1 faceless clip/day; DM tier-1 seeds with personal codes; pin
  #RetoTarima rules.
- **Wk 2-3:** repost every entry < 24h; first "creador de la semana" code (the
  creator films the on-camera reveal, not you); reply-clips to top comments.
- **Wk 4:** double down on whichever creator/format converts (installs-per-post).
- **Wk 5-6:** YT Shorts mirror of the best clips ("como entrenar freestyle en
  casa" is durable search); second collab with converting creators.

## The two things that decide success (unchanged, founder-independent)

1. **Installs-per-posted-video** — once one creator archetype clears a threshold,
   pour effort there. Needs the redeem-code attribution live.
2. **D7 retention** — instrument it before scaling. Faceless or not, virality
   into a leaky bucket is wasted. See the retention task in the run sheet.
