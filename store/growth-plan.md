# Tarima — growth & virality plan

Honest framing first: apps don't "go viral" because they're good. They grow when
the *content people make with them* is more watchable than the app itself. Tarima
has a rare advantage here: a freestyle session over a beat, with constraint words
appearing in real time, is inherently watchable even to people who'll never
install. The whole plan below is built around that one asset, the screen-recorded
session as a content unit, and a loop that turns watchers into makers.

The thing we are NOT doing: paid ads (no budget), buying followers, or waiting
for a "launch day" spike. Cold-start is solved by seeding makers, not by a press
push.

---

## 0. The core loop (everything serves this)

```
maker films a session  →  posts it (wants reposts/clout)  →  their audience sees
the words-on-beat format  →  some install to try it  →  they film a session  →  …
```

The UGC-for-Pro mechanic is the accelerant: "send us a clip we can repost, get
Tarima Pro free." It does three jobs at once:
1. Gives makers a *reason* to film and send (status + free Pro).
2. Feeds our own channels a constant stream of free, authentic content.
3. Lets us *track which makers actually drive installs* (via per-person codes),
   so we double down on the ones who convert.

If only one thing ships, it's this loop. The redeem-code system (separate doc /
build) is its infrastructure.

## 1. Audience & where they already are

Spanish freestyle is a real, dense culture, not a niche we're inventing:
- **Scenes:** FMS (España, Argentina, México, Chile, Perú, Colombia), Red Bull
  Batalla, plaza/competition culture, the legacy of El Quinto Escalón.
- **Who trains:** teens to mid-20s, LatAm + Spain, who watch batallas on YouTube
  and want to improve their own improv. Many practice alone in their room. That
  lonely-practice moment is exactly what Tarima fills.
- **Where they scroll:** TikTok first, IG Reels second, YouTube Shorts third.
  Spanish freestyle clips already pull millions on all three.

We meet them inside that culture with their language (gallo, tirar, plaza,
sangre, deja, minuto, palabra), not as "a productivity app for rappers."

## 2. Content engine (the daily reps)

One format, endless variations. Each is a 9:16 screen-recording of a session
(beat playing, words dropping) with a face-cam or voice over the top.

Proven-watchable angles:
- **"Palabra imposible"** — escalate difficulty until the word is absurd
  (`esternocleidomastoideo`) and they have to rhyme it. Comedy + skill.
- **Reto de racha** — don't drop a single bar for 16 bars; fail = restart.
- **Niveles** — same person, fácil → extremo, visible glow-up in 30s.
- **POV** — "POV: el último gallo de la plaza descubre que entrenas con esto."
- **Dúo / pásamela** — two people trade bars on the same word feed.
- **Reaction/stitch bait** — leave an obvious gap a stitcher can finish.

Cadence: aim for 1/day on TikTok during the first 6 weeks. Volume beats polish;
the algorithm rewards posting, and you have an infinite content source (the app
itself + reposts). Reuse: best TikTok → IG Reel → YT Short, same file.

## 3. The #RetoTarima challenge (the public face of the loop)

A named, repeatable challenge so participation is obvious and searchable:
- Rule: film yourself freestyling the words Tarima gives you on one beat, post
  with #RetoTarima and tag @tarimafreestyle.
- We repost the best ones daily (this is the reward, exposure).
- The best of the week gets a Pro code on camera (turns the reward into content).
- Weekly theme to keep it fresh (semana boom-bap, semana palabras imposibles,
  semana en inglés/spanglish).

A challenge with a hashtag + a repost engine is what converts passive watchers
into makers, which is the only thing that compounds.

## 4. Seeding makers (the cold-start fix)

You don't need fame, you need ~20-50 micro-creators making the format look
normal. Tiered outreach:
- **Tier 1 — micro freestylers (1k-50k):** plaza kids, regional battlers, freestyle
  meme accounts. DM the app + a personal Pro code + "film one, we'll repost you to
  our audience." Cheap, high willingness, they want exposure. This is 80% of effort.
- **Tier 2 — mid (50k-500k):** freestyle coaches, "aprende a rapear" accounts,
  beat producers. Offer Pro + co-made content (a niveles video with their face).
- **Tier 3 — known names:** only after the format is already circulating; pitch a
  one-off, not a deal. Don't burn these early.

Track every seeded creator's code (see redeem system): who posted, who drove
installs. Reallocate effort to whoever converts. Most won't; that's fine.

## 5. Platform tactics

- **TikTok:** primary. Hook in first 1s (word already on screen, beat hitting).
  Caption asks a question ("¿tú rimarías esta?"). Reply to every comment with a
  comment-video to multiply reach. Post 10-30s.
- **IG Reels:** repost TikToks (cover the watermark or re-export clean). Use the
  collab/repost feature with makers so it hits both audiences.
- **YouTube Shorts:** same files; YT search is durable, "como entrenar freestyle"
  is an evergreen query. Also keep one 3-5 min long-form ("cómo entreno freestyle
  en casa") as the SEO anchor that funnels to the app.
- **Reddit/Discord:** r/rapenespanol, freestyle Discords, plaza WhatsApp groups,
  share as a tool, not an ad. Founders posting authentically > brand posting.

## 6. ASO (so the installs from content actually land)

- Title: `Tarima: Freestyle y Rimas` (keyword in name).
- Keep "freestyle", "rimas", "rap", "beats", "batalla", "improvisar" across the
  short + full description (already drafted in `play-listing.md`).
- First screenshot must show the words-on-beat format, that's the differentiator,
  not a menu.
- Push for ratings: after a user completes ~5 good sessions, prompt once for a
  review (Play in-app review API). Ratings drive store ranking + conversion.

## 7. Metrics & targets (so we know if it's working)

Track weekly:
- Installs (Play console) + install source where visible.
- Codes issued vs redeemed vs which creator (our own attribution).
- Posts using #RetoTarima (manual count early).
- D1/D7 retention (does the app keep them? if not, content waste).
- Review count + avg rating.

Honest milestones, not vanity:
- Weeks 1-6: 30-50 seeded makers, format circulating, find 2-3 creators who
  actually convert. Success = a repeatable loop, not a number.
- The single number that matters: **installs-per-posted-video**. Once one creator
  archetype reliably clears a threshold, pour effort there.

## 8. 6-week run sheet

- **Wk 0 (pre-store):** finish redeem-code system; pre-make 15-20 session clips;
  build the seed list (50 micro creators with handles); write the DM template.
- **Wk 1:** soft post daily on TikTok from @tarimafreestyle; DM tier-1 seeds with
  personal codes; launch #RetoTarima with rules pinned.
- **Wk 2-3:** repost every decent #RetoTarima entry within 24h; reply-video to
  comments; first "creador de la semana" Pro-code-on-camera.
- **Wk 4:** double down on whichever format/creator converts; start tier-2 outreach
  with the proof from weeks 1-3.
- **Wk 5-6:** publish the long-form YT anchor; ask converting makers for a second
  collab; review which codes drove installs and prune the seed list.

## 9. What will actually kill this (watch for these)

- **Posting < daily.** The loop needs fuel; sporadic posting = no algorithm trust.
- **Reposting slowly.** If makers don't get reposted within a day, they stop
  sending. Repost speed IS the reward.
- **Bad retention.** If installs don't stick, virality just fills a leaky bucket.
  Fix D7 before scaling spend of any kind (even time).
- **Treating it like a brand account.** Founders' faces and real freestyle reps
  outperform polished brand content every time in this culture.
